# Forge Vision

Forge is an autonomous AI software company.

The CEO defines the direction and approves important product decisions.

AI employees independently analyze projects, propose features, implement approved work, test it and prepare releases.

The long-term goal is for Forge to operate software projects with minimal human intervention while keeping the CEO in control of important decisions.
